🌟 Helpful Duplicate Bridge Etiquette Reminders

✅ Call the Director Kindly: When an infraction occurs (lead out of turn, insufficient bid), raise your hand and say "Director, please!" in a friendly tone. Calling the director protects everyone's rights.
✅ Bidding Box Courtesy: Decide what you are going to bid before reaching for the bidding box to avoid hesitations and unauthorized information.
✅ Opening Lead Before Writing: On defense, make your opening lead face down before writing the contract on your score sheet or entering it into the electronic scoring tablet.
✅ Welcoming Atmosphere: Be encouraging to beginners and visitors. A smile and a friendly greeting make bridge a wonderful game for everyone!
