Understanding Masterpoint Colors

Black Points: Won at regular sanctioned club sessions like our weekly games.
Silver Points: Won at Sectional tournaments and Sectional Tournaments at Clubs (STaC).
Red Points: Won at Regional tournaments and special North American events.
Gold Points: Won in qualifying flighted events at Regional tournaments and NABCs.
