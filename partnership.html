Guaranteed Standby Host Program

On Saturday 11:00 AM Open Pairs games, VBC provides a dedicated standby host player. If you show up at the club 15 minutes before game time without a partner, you are guaranteed a seat!

No advance reservation necessary on Saturdays—just come ready to play.
